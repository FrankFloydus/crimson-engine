# Quader task list

## Serious open defects

Reviewed 2026-09-05. Keep only verified serious open defects; remove an item after its fix and
regression are verified. Durable invariants belong in [memory.md](memory.md) or the owner guide.
This is a broad source audit, not a proof of every line. Optional features and style work are
excluded from this defect section.

No verified serious open findings remained from that 2026-09-05 audit. Subsequent
verified findings follow.

- [ ] **Bulk component selection, dragging and commit still stall on dense scenes**
  (verified 2026-09-28). The real Vulkan viewport fixture at 1280×720 selects 42,892
  visible vertices or 81,955 edges across 5,441 meshes in a clone of `one.qdr`.
  The capture-5 pass measures 212 ms median complete vertex-drag frames, 467 ms
  initial selection and 626 ms commit. Repeated camera-dependent marquee queries
  reuse all document facts but still take about 385 ms including overlay rebuilding.
  Selected-vertex navigation takes 7.4 ms before the edit and 14.8 ms afterward;
  planned primitive submissions grow from 1,068 to 2,934 as geometry becomes unique.
  Avoidable instance fragmentation after compatible geometry merges is fixed, but
  generic batching of genuinely distinct meshes remains absent. Evidence:
  `build/component5-frame-vertex-after.log` and `build/tracy-component-analysis-5/findings.md`,
  generated by [the native viewport fixture](tests/dense_component_frame_runtime_tests.cpp).
  Authoritative candidate preparation and affected normal neighborhoods now share work;
  the separate CPU-only deformation fixture is not a substitute for these frame timings.
  Owners: [native preview projection](src/editor/modeling/services/modeling_sdk_projection_adapter.cpp),
  [mesh position projection](src/editor/projection/mesh_projection_service.cpp), and
  [retained preview submission](src/render/tool_preview_renderable_cache.cpp), plus
  [scene extraction](src/render/scene_render_extraction.cpp) and
  [overlay publication](src/render/overlay/overlay_renderable_cache.cpp).
  Acceptance: the same-scene viewport workload sustains interactive drag cadence and
  bounds selection/start/commit stalls, while preserving canonical geometry, visible
  overlays, commit/cancel and undo/redo. Report total frames and individual phases;
  retain [projection parity](tests/uv_native_sampling_tests.cpp) and
  [component persistence](tests/component_transform_persistence_tests.cpp) coverage.
