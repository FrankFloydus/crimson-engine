# Directional seams, surface fog and depth precision

Three different faults were reproduced: sharp solar radiance in surface fog, filtered directional-shadow leakage at finite joins, and a hidden floor winning the camera depth test over a nearer wall. Wall thickness alone cannot repair the latter two sampling problems. Diagnostics use the saved room plus independent closed-room and finite-opening controls.

The first finite-geometry supplement also caused a serious GPU and shader-compilation regression. The performance correction separates that work from material shaders and evaluates one camera-local visibility field with shared source-ray traversal. The original interior now has zero RGB direct-light leakage, with a byte-identical lit unshadowed control. Prototype measurements below are retained separately from the final shared-pass results.

## Sharp solar disk in surface fog

Surface aerial perspective previously sampled mip-zero environment radiance. The sky's sharp solar disk therefore became fog color on an opaque wall. Surface fog now selects filtered radiance by view depth, from the coarsest near mip toward at least the first roughness mip in the distance. Visible sky retains its own source.

The GPU regression uses sentinel cubemap mips and checks near/far surfaces, Receive Fog, disabled aerial perspective and visible sky. Aerial Perspective and Sun Scatter remain artistic unshadowed haze. Proper wall-occluded light shafts require evaluating visibility through a volume; multiplying surface fog by a surface shadow is not a valid substitute.

## Per-texel shadow comparisons

The optimized PCF previously shared one comparison reference across bilinear texels. Each of the 36 contributing raw texels now receives its own receiver-plane reference. Equivalent separable weights remain `[1-f, 3-2f, 4-f, 3+f, 1+2f, f] / 12`. PCSS disk taps reconstruct four individually compared texels. Kernel support, blocker search, authored bias, cascade fitting, overlap continuity and far fading are preserved.

An independent perpendicular-plane calculation reproduced .0978472 false visibility with the old reference and zero with per-texel references. Expanded ideal kernels agree to roughly 1e-15 across 505 controls. A grazing closed-box control nevertheless retained 224 bright join pixels at 1024 shadow resolution, down from 499. This correction improves the estimate; it cannot recover arbitrary finite polygon coverage from a single shadow depth layer.

## Camera depth correction

GPU Normal captures identified a separate residual floor strip. Some pixels shaded the hidden floor even though a closest-surface ray found a nearer wall. At the saved Scene near plane of approximately .0006994 m and a join around 36 m away, ordinary D24 spacing was about .11 m. Those nearby surfaces could compete in one depth bin. This is a raster-winner problem, not evidence that the floor's authored normal or the wall thickness is wrong.

Rich Scene/Game rendering now uses direct reversed floating projections, D32FS8 main depth/stencil, D32F readable/picking depth, clear zero and GREATER/GEQUAL comparisons. The maintained bgfx port adds D32FS8 and advances the fork API to 130/port15. SSGI/SSAO inverse projections, raw-depth background checks, temporal precision tolerances and overlay offsets follow this convention. SSR Hi-Z and refraction remain positive linear view-depth comparisons. Shadow maps retain their ordinary depth convention. CPU authoring rays and CoreModeling projection remain independent.

Authored near/far planes are preserved. A color-only discard of the wrong floor would leave depth, GI, SSAO and SSR inconsistent and was not adopted.

## Finite geometric visibility

Immutable original-LOD0 triangle hierarchies are prepared on resource workers. Per-view occurrence hierarchies retain affine transforms, caster masks, sidedness and actual camera eligibility. Shared geometry is deduplicated in a disjoint row-aligned Forward+ atlas range; unchanged data reuses CPU/GPU publication. Posed, skinned, morphed, faded, masked, transparent, dynamically covered and actively displaced geometry does not supply supplemental opaque casters.

Sun queries extend through the complete finite eligible scene bounds. Camera validation stops at the actual near plane. Bounds rejection never treats a box as an occluder. A hard-source full-coverage proof requires one finite triangle to strictly cover the four pixel corners and actual center. Ambiguous footprints retain 32 deterministic subpixel/angular samples. Confirmed camera-hidden tangent samples are removed from both numerator and denominator; zero-valid footprints retain CSM. Shadow-only geometry cannot invalidate camera samples.

The triangle predicate includes shared-edge error handling, a near-zero distance bound and an independent difference-first plane-distance certificate. This distinguishes uncertain coplanar self-hits from a genuinely nearby different wall without a second receiver-sized distance cutoff. Geometry visibility combines with CSM using their minimum before the existing far fade, avoiding double attenuation of the same blocker. Finite openings and physical edge coverage remain regression controls.

The performance implementation evaluates the supplement once per view from exact FP32 raster receiver position and plane. A detached full-resolution scratch field publishes four directional values into its own atlas interval. Eligible full-opacity primary opaque/masked mesh occurrences perform an exact integer-pixel lookup; Alpha/faded meshes, sprites, previews/thumbnails and unavailable inputs retain ordinary CSM. An exact normal-compatibility flag permits a safe CSM-zero early-out; unmatched normals still evaluate geometry. Material shaders no longer contain the heavy traversal. There is no temporal reconstruction or bilinear borrowing from neighboring receivers.

For hard sources, the 32 source rays share one hierarchy traversal per receiver. Each candidate triangle is fetched once and tested against unresolved sample bits using the original source reconstruction and triangle predicate. Conservative shared-parameter slabs, finite-triangle rejection and strict single-triangle coverage proofs bound that work. Positive angular diameter uses independent narrow ray traversals with the same sample directions; the later angular-source investigation below records why. Camera validation still conditions the same samples individually. Invalid packet traversal discards its result and uses the independent-ray fallback. A tile-classification prototype was removed after isolated measurements showed its cost exceeded the work it avoided.

This is finite SM5 software traversal, not a complete hardware ray-tracing backend or exact continuous area-light integral. Capacity/address failures retain CSM. That implementation checkpoint used material ABI35/generator36; the current ABI and per-input source revisions are recorded in the architecture. Twelve graph textures and six-texel point records remain coherent; reserved uniform lanes carry the supplemental metadata. Woop et al. guide the shared-edge predicate, but without their double fallback the same formal watertightness guarantee is not claimed.
