# Crimson Engine repository instructions

Native Windows x64 first-person action game engine and editor: C++20, MSVC, CMake, SDL3, bgfx with Direct3D 12 by default and optional Direct3D 11, and Dear ImGui docking. The official renderer target/executable is `crimson-renderer` / `crimson-renderer.exe`.

## Current game direction

- The game now uses first-person movement and an editor-authored eye camera. DUSK and Quake are references for movement and camera feel: WASD, direct mouse look, jumping and lateral camera tilt. Keep new gameplay and level work consistent with that perspective.
- The former isometric ARPG player recipe and click-to-move input have been replaced. Retain precise legacy scene conversion and custom authored cameras; do not reintroduce the old player mode. Enemy and externally commanded actor navigation remain independent reusable systems.
- Actors, collision, navigation, gameplay tags, Attributes and Gameplay Effects remain implemented foundations. Their presence does not define weapons, combat, inventory, loot or progression rules for the new game; implement those only within an agreed feature slice.
- Historical D2R and ARPG renderer checkpoints remain evidence for existing code and measurements. They do not set the current game scope or require a fixed camera, a particular player-light rig, or the rest of the reference game's pipeline. Use the current architecture and lighting guide for implemented behavior and limits.

## Current implementation contracts

These contracts describe the implementation as of 2026-10-25. Preserve them when changing the relevant system; consult the linked architecture sections for full details instead of reconstructing decisions from old checkpoints or experiments.

### First-person player, camera and stairs

- `runtime/GameInput` owns physical WASD, relative mouse look and Space jump edges while Game owns input. `PlaySession` owns transient look, strafe tilt and camera presentation; `physics/CharacterMotor` / `PhysicsWorld` own the collision-resolved capsule. Player input requires no Navigation Agent, volume or bake. Movement speed comes from resolved `Stats.MovementSpeed`; acceleration, friction, air control, jump height and view response belong to `MovementComponent`, presented as Movement directly after Transform.
- Preserve the [project Jolt overlay](cmake/vcpkg-ports/joltphysics/README.md), which backports opposing-contact stair probes into pinned Jolt 5.5.0. Both probes follow the blocking terrain riser independently of camera/body facing, including A/D strafing and oblique spiral-stair approaches. Virtual characters remain blockers in every sweep but cannot select the riser direction; their requested velocities differ from Crimson's no-pushing contact policy. The contact must oppose movement; profile step/slope limits and capsule clearance still apply. Change the overlay and rebuild through the wrapper; never edit extracted dependencies under `build/`.
- The Player's real child/follow camera remains editable in the Inspector and through camera authoring. Preserve its authored offset, lens and affine basis; runtime look, tilt and stair compensation affect only the interpolated presentation camera, never saved transforms, follow settings or history. The First Person template uses a 1.65 m eye offset, 75° vertical FOV, 0.05 m near and 2048 m far planes.
- Stair smoothing uses the complete supported vertical terrain correction, including capsule rounding and settling outside Jolt's stair helpers. Exclude continuous travel on the actual support plane, upward jumps, unsupported falls and landing ticks. Keep native Jolt `ExtendedUpdate` traversal; do not alter physical movement to disguise camera jitter. Presentation samples the critically damped return analytically between fixed ticks and cancels terrain displacement at the same interpolation fraction. Retain full accumulated compensation and the world-collision camera-clearance guard; a fixed 0.5 m offset cap reintroduces jolts on closely spaced stairs.
- **Movement → Stair Smoothing (s)** authors `stairSmoothingTime` in [0,1], default 0.10 s. Lower is faster, higher is softer, and zero clears pending compensation immediately even without a physics tick. Positive live edits preserve current look and compensation. Camera/player identity, authored pose/profile edits, transform locks, suspension and Play teardown reset transient state. Preserve 60 Hz fixed simulation, responsive render-frame mouse look and explicit Scene/Game input ownership.
- **Player Input → Trace Test → Hit Impulse (N s)** authors optional schema-24 `traceTest.hitImpulse`, default 50, finite in [0,100000]. F traces from the final eye camera even without a physics tick and tests compatible assigned ragdolls' posed bone colliders with the authored query mask and nearer solid occlusion. A hit activates the model from its current pose and applies impulse only to the struck body at the contact along the normalized trace; repeated hits use the active ragdoll. Zero activates without a kick. Preserve one press edge, Player/owned-attachment exclusions, trigger exclusion, missing-load defaults without writes, transactional reset/undo/cancel and Play isolation.

### Reusable Jolt ragdolls

- `assets/RagdollAsset` owns `.crimson-ragdoll` v1 and exact source-model/skin/reference-pose signatures. Up to 256 physical bodies bind distinct skin joints; parents are the nearest selected ancestors in parent-first order. Geometry/mass and child-reference joint anchors/rotations/degree limits are authored data. Generic capsule generation is a starting fit, independent of humanoid names. Empty unfinished rigs are valid. Source changes require explicit undoable per-body remapping, never silent bone reassignment; unrigged meshes remain unsupported.
- `editor/RagdollDocument` and `tools/RagdollEditor` own independent shared File/Edit/history/save/conflict flows. Keep Bones in a full-height left sidebar with compact tree indentation, the preview in the center, and a resizable right sidebar split horizontally between simulation properties above and Ragdoll/Body/Joint properties below. Skeleton and Remap sections are removed; exact source validation and backend remapping APIs remain intact. Preview camera, clip/time, drop height, floor and Simulate/Pause/Reset remain transient. The isolated world uses fixed 60 Hz simulation. Left-drag ray-picks a physical body and updates a native constraint target; release/focus loss/Escape/pause/reset/source change releases it. Preserve body/joint wires, selected limits, standard navigation and document/input ownership. Canonical RagdollPreview owns targets/environment and unused views 416-429, with ordinary skins, tone mapping, application antialiasing and grid; it omits Glow/SSR/scene-color refraction capture and leaves existing numeric IDs/native terminal copies intact.
- Optional schema-24 `SceneModelRagdoll {asset,startSimulated}` belongs to each model occurrence; absent is empty/off and loading writes nothing. Model Inspector and browser actions create/open/assign reusable assets with reference/history/deletion support. `PlaySession` activates/resets/impulses per-model ragdolls from the current animation pose and actor velocity, yielding owned actor movement/navigation. One active full ragdoll per Actor/Controller owner is supported. Source/assignment/placement/owner changes and transform locks invalidate stale bodies; Stop clears transient state. Scene/Game share the interpolated immutable skin pose and conservative bounds through existing deformation, shadow and picking consumers.
- `PhysicsWorld` owns Jolt ragdolls, shapes, swing/twist joints, impulses, body mappings and drag constraints, and steps native dynamics once per fixed tick. Character inner collision proxies support dynamic contacts while explicit virtual-character peer/no-pushing behavior remains intact. Model-world scale must be positive uniform without shear/reflection; bone reference frames must be nonsingular/right-handed. Passive full-rig ragdolls are implemented; active pose motors, partial animation/physics blending, balance/get-up recovery, general dynamic Collider authoring and animated triangle-mesh collision remain separate slices.
- `editor/RagdollAuthoring` owns exact posed-collider picking, bone-local proxy conversion and shape-aware resizing; `tools/RagdollBodyGizmo` owns stopped-preview T/R/E gestures with immutable baselines, one undo command and exact cancellation. Use editor-focused shortcuts for Move/Rotate/Resize without operation radio buttons; active fields and popups retain input ownership. ImGuizmo uses separate editor IDs: Scene must not disable or manipulate a foreign active gizmo. Preview Test Mode fixes all bodies outside Whole/Selected Body/Selected Branch scope before ticking; manual pins freeze the current pose through native kinematic bodies, retaining every joint. Pin/unpin restores dynamics without authored writes; pinned bodies stay selectable but reject mouse constraints/impulses. Scope and pins never enter asset/scene/history data.

### Renderer backends and shader publication

- Renderer implementation belongs in the canonical `https://github.com/qvertexworks/crimson-renderer.git` repository, consumed here through `third_party/crimson-renderer`. Publish completed renderer changes on shared `main`, then commit the Engine's matching submodule pin and host integration. Do not leave completed shared work only in a dirty consumer checkout, use a sibling renderer copy, or override the submodule URL with a local filesystem path.
- Direct3D 12 is the application default. **Edit → Preferences → Rendering → Renderer (restart required)** selects DX12 or DX11 for the next launch; `--renderer dx11|dx12` overrides it for one process without saving. `GraphicsBackend.h`, `EditorPreferences`, `app/Options` and `App` own selection before bgfx initialization. Backend choice is application data, never a scene/Environment property. The Engine rich Forward+/PBR path does not implement Vulkan. The shared renderer separately implements borrowed Vulkan for Quader's modeling/editor path.
- **Rendering → Antialiasing** selects Off/SMAA live, default SMAA. The host supplies this presentation preference through `SceneSettings::antiAliasing` to Scene/Game and material/particle previews; asset cards use fixed SMAA. Shared `core::Smaa` implements reference SMAA 1x High after tone mapping and before editor overlays with explicit edges/weights/blend passes, linear reconstruction and one final encoding. RawData and picking remain unfiltered. Temporal AA/upscaling and offscreen MSAA remain unimplemented. Preserve material ABI 39 and focused `smaa_render_core`/DX11 checks.
- Both APIs use the same SM5/DXBC shader family and material cache contract. The historical `dx11` shader build token does not mean the running renderer is DX11. Keep embedded programs, shader manifests, generated material interfaces, reload validation and cache generator identity coherent; never edit generated headers manually. See [material shader startup](docs/MATERIAL_SHADER_STARTUP.md).
- Preserve the [startup/browser regression requirements](docs/MATERIAL_SHADER_STARTUP.md#startup-and-browser-regression-requirements). Source revisions and host recipe contracts are pass-specific: a Decal-only change must leave unrelated compiled material artifacts reusable. The build warmer publishes a complete usable catalog before cold O3 surface/domain optimization, using runtime's shared interactive policy; warm repeats require zero compiler processes. Cross-process cold requests share only their exact input through a cancellable lease and recheck the cache after acquiring it. Cache-only presentation never waits on that lease. Browser source deduplication and GPU fingerprint validation share canonical Windows path identity. Ready Interactive/last-good packets remain visible while optional work continues; no whole-scene or command-disable gate. Run the actual fixed/generated/Blend/Decal thumbnail cold-and-disk-restore regressions, selective invalidation, first-use catalog and concurrency checks for changes to these contracts. Do not hand off until the Release wrapper's cache preparation finishes.
- Scene thumbnail decal failures use typed terminal state and the existing bounded thumbnail retry backoff. A stale failure diagnostic or snapshot during an active reload is pending, not terminal; partial usable patches remain successful. Dependency invalidation clears retry state. Keep `decal_spatial_cache_core` failed/reloading/pending cases, `preview_dependencies_core`, and the actual-owner `decal_thumbnail_retry_render_core`/DX11 recovery checks live when changing this path; two permanently failed scene cards must not repeat their full preparation every frame.
- Material ABI is 39; the independent fragment, node-preview and native-domain source revisions in canonical `MaterialGraphSourceRevisions.h` currently all equal 42. This is not a global invalidation stamp. The family retains explicit None/Circular/Quad UV0 interpolation, the PointShadow prior-world varying/optional rigid-motion depth-prepass MRT and five surface color outputs, including the unfogged SSR source. Opt-in Lit/Translucent refraction adds adjacent transmission/surface draws, preserving twelve graph textures through separate scene-color12/environment13/readable-depth14 transmission bindings. It samples one immutable opaque-plus-sky snapshot; transparent layers never enter that source. The superseded emission-only Glow MRT and `u_glowSource` are removed. Preserve `u_clusterRecordLayoutV6`, six texels per point record and the 27-float/108-byte mesh layout. Update every affected producer/consumer and cache identity together when changing that ABI. Tone-curve coefficients are presentation-only and stay outside the generated-material ABI.
- Material Output **Height** and **Tessellation** were introduced in graph schema8 inside native material v6; the current writer uses schema20, readers accept graph schemas 1–20 and missing fields default off without writes on load. DX12 mesh color/depth/picking/point-shadow/directional-shadow and transmission variants share native hull/domain displacement; DX11, node previews and particles retain ordinary geometry. Preserve explicit Linear Data height sampling, clamp Height to 0–1, mesh-local signed Amount (default 0.1), Midpoint 0.5, uniform Factor 8 (1–64) and explicit Height LOD 0 (0–16). Disabling retains links and excludes active Height work. Enabled meshes request original LOD0 and expand conservative local bounds before the full affine transform. Dynamic Height invalidates relevant shadow content and unsupported GI history; collision stays CPU geometry. Keep native domain recipes/manifests, four-stage publication, embedded stages, affected source revisions and ABI 39 coherent.
- Preserve the pinned vcpkg baseline and the [project bgfx overlay](third_party/crimson-renderer/cmake/vcpkg-ports/bgfx/README.md). `d3d12-texture-upload.patch` fixes small-mip upload descriptors and staging written ranges; `d3d12-timer-fence.patch` waits for the command list containing timestamp resolution. Modify the overlay sources and rebuild through the wrapper, not extracted dependencies under `build/`.
- `PlatformSurface` and `ImGuiBgfx` own native secondary-window presentation. DX12 resources require both bgfx-wrapper retirement and confirmed queue completion; the marker after `Present` protects backbuffer reuse/resize/close. A timeout is not proof of completion. Preserve bounded allocator reuse, queued retirement and accounting. Do not reorder numeric view IDs casually: readbacks, compute-before-graphics sorting and the terminal platform-copy boundary depend on them.
- [The backend comparison](docs/RENDERER_BACKEND_COMPARISON.md) records the measured workloads and limitations. DX12 is not universally faster, and timestamp elapsed time is not active GPU execution time. Renderer multithreading was explicitly deferred by the user; existing resource preparation workers remain in place. Do not add a threading rewrite or rerun performance suites as routine handoff work.

### Blend materials and vertex paint

- `MaterialShading::BlendPbr` supplies Base/R/G/B typed Surface references to existing direct Lit/Opaque materials without enabled tessellation or nested Material References. Graph schema20/native material v6 retain earlier numeric identities. Optional per-reference Height maps, height remapping/blending, PBR channel influences, tint/roughness and mapping overrides retain the default flat blend and original scene paint data. Height changes appearance independently of geometric tessellation. CPU source expansion consumes immutable committed snapshots, preserves cached-image origins and shares twelve samplers/208 vec4 rows. Saved/open/history dependencies include inactive references; runtime demand follows active inputs. Parent culling/fog, child PBR blending and one imported tint/lighting evaluation must remain coherent across previews, thumbnails, world draws, deletion and the cache commandlet.
- Canonical `forward/MeshPaint` owns Hammer-style corner-quad refinement at levels0–5, logical quad interpolation, source provenance and immutable RGB8 COW pages. Preserve original corners and planar shape without relaxation. Known quads remain logical quads; unknown triangle topology gets corner patches. GPU triangles use inverse-bilinear paint interpolation through a separate 44-byte stream, retaining the primary 108-byte layout. Source signatures and topology recipes are distinct from paint appearance. Per-occurrence/view/draw GPU owners compare actual RGB controls inside replaced COW pages, update incident cells through at most four coalesced shadow-buffer ranges, request original LOD0 and exclude instancing. Occluder visibility changes must not discard selected projection/seam/footprint caches.
- Scene schema25 owns optional per-occurrence `SceneMeshPaint` (Level, recipe, per-source-draw signature/count/RGB8 payload). Loading writes nothing. Level edits resample current paint as one undoable edit; incompatible sources preserve payloads until explicit Reset Paint. `MeshPaintResources` uses existing workers; color/depth/picking/shadows/decals/Scene/Game/thumbnails agree on derived geometry. Collision stays separately authored.
- `VertexPaintTool` and `VertexPaintGesture` own selected rigid static mesh painting in Material mode, named source-material rows/previews, one stroke transaction, normalized replacement weights with stroke-local fractional accumulation before RGB8 publication, radius-relative world-space dabs, 32-pixel bins and ordered batches of eight dabs. Base also erases visible logical-cell contributions through a bounded footprint cache and paint-aware corner scatter, including controls outside the radius; validate sampled cell visibility before fractional or authored changes, combine overlapping effects once per dab, and preserve saved topology/paint plus immediate release. Shared controls retain their neighboring-cell influence. `MeshPaintResources` prepares immutable surface BVHs on existing workers; exact CPU rays handle opaque geometry and conservative unsupported coverage/posed bounds require rendered-surface verification through the sparse ROI cache. Unknown/stale visibility publishes no partial batch; preflight the first dab before taking stroke ownership. Handle release before drawing menus/panels; commit published work and cancel pending queries immediately. Paint must not gray the Outliner, Inspector or brush controls; finish the stroke before a conflicting command takes document ownership. Ordinary selection invalidation must not cancel the paint tool's GPU request. Other transaction guards remain intact; Escape/context/source/focus changes cancel exactly. Project the actual world-radius tangent circle separately from the conservative bin bound. The transient Show Subdivisions toggle overlays selected rigid meshes' logical cell perimeters through lazily prepared worker geometry and the Scene depth-tested wire pass; omit quad triangulation diagonals, include non-Blend slots, and never write display state into paint or scenes. Hold Scene preview animation/particles/material presentation time during a stroke and GPU surface preparation to keep cached occluders stable; hover preparation owns no editor commands. Thumbnail jobs pause during held strokes. Preserve explicit lifetime and history bounds; do not claim measured performance from this design. Skin/morph/animated/ragdoll painting, transparent blends and displacement painting remain outside this slice.

### Screen-space lighting, fog and presentation

- Scene point/directional lights support retained Color/Temperature modes (1000–15000 K), 65 independent fixture recipes and 24 single-behavior flicker presets. `scene/LightAuthoring` owns CPU conversion, deterministic sampling and relative intensity/color/radius channels. `runtime/SceneLights` owns scene-wide transient Edit Preview/Pause/Restart; Play owns a separate accepted-time clock. Extraction resolves presentation once for Scene/Game and the linked procedural sun, without authored writes. Optional scene-v24 fields default Color/6500 K/flicker off; preserve inactive mode values, strict validation, atomic history and Quader source-RGB ownership while retaining same-kind flicker. See [the lighting guide](docs/RENDERER_AND_CHARACTER_LIGHTING.md#light-presets-temperature-and-flickering).

- `EnvironmentSettings::specularAA` is an opt-in default-off Environment Lighting control, independent of preview settings. Keep optional strict-boolean persistence, undo/redo, missing-load cleanliness, thumbnail fingerprints and shared `u_pbrSettings(enabled,.15,.2,0)` coherent across fixed/generated lighting, SSR and glass. Material ABI39/source generator42 retains twelve graph slots; glass transmission uses its free BRDF-atlas slot15 to share the surface's hemispherical reflection budget.

- `EnvironmentSettings::ssr` owns optional current-frame SSR on Lit Opaque/Masked/Translucent mesh and sprite receivers. Keep unfogged opaque-plus-sky donors separate from post-SSR refraction capture, transparent appearance, fog, Glow and presentation. Reflection-only geometry retains graph0–11/source12/environment13/min-max-depth14/BRDF-AO-atlas15, signed environment replacement, own receiver normals/coverage and foreground fog transmittance once. Opaque/masked receivers capture FP32 position/normal-roughness/plane into Raw/Guide/A; shared `cs_ssr_trace` reads them and writes B, then reconstruction reads/writes B→Raw→A→B before exact-material composition. Keep three color attachments followed by scene depth/stencil, detached per-dispatch reads/writes and compact compute bindings 0–5. Preserve full-resolution deterministic 1/4/12 visible-GGX rays with 64/128/256 Hi-Z budgets per ray, four refinement steps, conservative odd mip footprints, depth-guided mip-zero opaque radiance, one-sided level-zero acceptance and exhausted-ray fallback. Receiver-plane self-hit rejection stays at mip zero; empty intervals may ascend. Scene/Game/previews/thumbnails own independent inputs; disabled/zero-strength SSR records no work. Glass reflection remains independent of Transmission and uses three adjacent draws. Alpha/glass and runtime-faded opaque/masked meshes retain direct inline tracing; material ABI39/source generator42 stay unchanged, and `cs_ssr_trace` participates in named-program initialization/reload and embedded-source validation. Four detached RGBA32F images cost 64 bytes/pixel, about 126.6 MiB at 1080p, or about 205.7 MiB including the source pyramids/scratch. Do not claim material-compilation or GPU-time improvements without matching measurements. No temporal history, transparent donors, offscreen recovery or planar reflections are implemented.
- Scene/Game and thumbnails use four stable directional cascades with a 0.8 logarithmic/uniform split mix, texel snapping, split blending and far fading. Shadow Angular Diameter defaults to 0.53° (0–2°); zero uses weighted 5×5 PCF, positive values use bounded contact-hardening PCSS. Preserve the common world halo, upstream offscreen casters, receiver-plane correction and continuous world normal bias across overlaps. The former single Game world/player-focus policy is removed. Point-shadow admission/update priority uses the visible Game camera eye, otherwise the Scene camera eye; freeze and reuse that origin through preparation and rendering. See [the directional contract](docs/RENDERER_AND_CHARACTER_LIGHTING.md#directional-shadows-for-first-person-cameras).
- Finite directional contact visibility uses shared beam traversal only for a hard source; positive angular diameter uses the same 32 deterministic rays with per-ray BVH slabs. A broad angular packet can visit nearly every triangle at a close-up receiver and cause hundreds of milliseconds of GPU work. Do not infer universal packet performance from a single saved-room camera. Preserve the finite offscreen caster range, exact camera-validity denominator and finite-opening coverage; compare close-up soft-source timing and pixels against the independent-ray control. Contact shader dependencies are separate from generated material inputs, so this change must not invalidate material families. Connected captures record actual per-view source angles, pixel count and caster-triangle count.
- `EnvironmentSettings::ssgi` and `::ssao`, shared `EnvironmentSerialization.h`, and the Environment Inspector own the opt-in effects. SSGI is the only GI method: the experimental world-space/dynamic GI method was removed. Missing effects default off; obsolete `gi` data converts in memory and writers emit only current `ssgi`/`ssao`. Loading must not rewrite or dirty scenes, and optional controls do not require a scene-version bump.
- `SsgiGpu` traces at half resolution with 1–4 screen-space diffuse bounces, temporal reprojection, independent raw luminance moments/history age, short-history variance reconstruction, filtering at steps 1/2/4 and atlas publication. Only the first spatial stage feeds next-frame irradiance history; wider stages remain display reconstruction. Medium retains four rays/sixteen steps, Stability .90 and authored denoise radius 4, whose three stages span seven half-resolution pixels. Scene and Game have separate histories and explicit resource owners; disabled/zero-strength effects schedule no effect work. Preserve history resets on view interruptions, identities, cuts, resize, settings and shaders. This is local visible-surface transport: it cannot provide Godot SDFGI-style offscreen room fill. Previews and thumbnails omit SSGI/SSAO.
- GI depth prepasses publish RGBA16F prior-minus-current world displacement/validity for rigid and instanced mesh occurrences. Primary receivers and donor hits use their own motion. New/incompatible geometry/materials, runtime-changing graph inputs and skin/morph deformation publish invalid motion and reject receiver history; unsupported donors retain geometry-validated camera-only lookup. Large normal changes conservatively reject history. Known authored material/light/environment source changes cap history age at four without resetting stationary receivers on moving-donor hits. Do not treat this bounded reconstruction as full A-SVGF/NRD or temporal AA.
- GI donors capture shadowed diffuse environment/direct lighting and eligible Lit emission, with separate World/Character lanes. Specular, existing GI, fog and Glow never enter donors. Opaque/cutout coverage and deformation must agree across shading/depth/capture; transparent draws preserve opaque auxiliary data, and partially faded models neither supply nor receive GI. Publication uses the existing Forward+ atlas, retaining all twelve material texture bindings.
- Scene directional/point lights expose **GI Energy** (`indirectEnergy`, default 1, range 0–16). It scales only that light's diffuse donor contribution, independently of direct brightness/specular/shadows. SSAO darkens environment/ambient and GI, not direct light or emission. Keep tunable quality/ray/step/denoising controls authored rather than replacing them with scene-specific constants. Full controls and limits are in [the lighting guide](docs/RENDERER_AND_CHARACTER_LIGHTING.md).
- Scene/Game top-right debug choices are Lit, Albedo, Roughness, Metal, Normal, Direct Lighting and Indirect Lighting, owned by transient `RenderDebugView` state. Toolbar gestures stay outside viewport input ownership. Lighting diagnostics retain exposure/tone mapping: ACES can clip a weak isolated indirect signal to black. Use Linear presentation when investigating that case before concluding point-light capture is broken.
- Fog covers the empty background/sky as well as finite surfaces. Exponential Density 0 with Height Density 0 must yield exact clear coverage, including the background. Preserve Sky Affect, Aerial Perspective and Sun Scatter through the shared environment serializer and shader paths; Crimson deliberately uses density-aware sky transmittance, rather than Godot's density-independent sky opacity. Material Receive Fog remains independent of lighting and coverage. The editor grid/tools compose later and remain exposure independent.
- `ToneMapping` owns authored per-operator controls shared by Scene/Game presentation and the CPU reference. Preserve linear HDR lighting and one final sRGB encoding. Changing the tone curve, exposure or diagnostic mode must not change GI source energy or direct-light settings. One full-scene HDR Glow effect includes emission, reflected/direct highlights and visible sky/sun. Additive/Mix/Replace compose in HDR; Screen/Soft Light compose in linear presentation space before SMAA. Glow exposes intensity, blend mode, spread, tint, highlight threshold, seven independent linear level gains, saturation, HDR Scale, HDR Luminance Cap, anamorphic stretch and quality. Never normalize current gains by their sum; each level must fade continuously to zero. Old separate Glow/Bloom settings convert once in memory into current `glow` with `source: "Scene"`; writers omit Bloom and Core/Halo Balance. Loading never rewrites or dirties authored data. Spread/stretch select filtered base extent, never sparse tent-tap spacing. Preserve progressive source prefilter reductions of at most 2:1 per axis (up to five prefilters plus seven weighted levels/six up passes); default High starts half resolution and Low quarter, while narrow Spread can use full resolution. Disabled/zero Glow schedules no effect work. Temporal AA and DLSS/FSR remain unimplemented.

### Surface and Box PBR decals

- `src/decals/` owns immutable original-LOD sources/BVHs, oriented contact topology, source-triangle barycentrics, seed resolution, intrinsic charts and derived disk caches. Select the seed-connected original-boundary component before subdivision while retaining finite interior-contact cuts and ambiguity barriers; coplanar overlaps retain multiplicity checks. Establish the common grid before local contact cuts, then insert shared boundary endpoints before conforming convex-cell triangulation. Exact developable unfolding requires agreeing cycles/metrics; nondevelopable patches use bounded exact polyhedral geodesics over the original embedded metric, with stable difference-first areas, angles and source intervals. There is no planar/triplanar substitution. Separate receiver meshes retain their IDs, geometry, slots and collision. Gaps and ambiguous branches, including disconnected bones within one mesh, stop traversal rather than create connector geometry. Chart and preview UVs map local +Y toward raster V=0 at the image top; preserve explicit authored rotations/mirrors without flipping source images or saved transforms.
- `.crimson-decal` v2 stores a typed native material reference. Shader Model Decal supplies dedicated Color/Opacity/Normal/Roughness/Metallic and per-channel opacity outputs through generated pass9, preserving twelve graph samplers; ordinary mesh/particle fields accept Lit/Unlit only. Version-1 map assets convert to deterministic staged graph materials without writes; explicit Save materializes the companion and current decal together. Material and decal documents retain independent transactional history. Preview receiver meshes include plane, sphere, cylinder, cube and rounded cube, with chart preparation on existing ResourceManager workers.
- Decal receiver selection is generic and bounded by the footprint, independent of import source. Optional entity Receive Decals defaults true; persist false only, preserve history/reimport/Play isolation, and omit retired Quader scopes/includes/excludes/source-key fields. Authored affine Transform is placement authority; derive a tangent chart frame preserving its XY metric, shear and reflection, and reproject locally across draw/entity boundaries after movement. Decal editing uses Position, surface-normal Rotation and Width/Height in metres with two-dimensional Lock proportions; a single-decal gizmo uses local normal rotation and X/Y footprint handles. ### Recurring failures: diagnose the owning stage

- A straight subdivision grid depends on source polygon topology, not the displayed triangle wireframe. Preserve native Quader face boundaries through import and inspect logical cells with Show Subdivisions. Do not infer quads from arbitrary triangle pairs. Paint is normalized per-control RGB8 material weighting, not a texture mask; interpolation and shared controls explain coarse-cell influence outside a tiny brush. Base's bounded cell scatter must not become a global erase or bypass visibility checks.
- For a decal failure, distinguish conservative BVH candidates, accepted source triangles, analysis/common-subdivision work and final output. Reproduce the saved seed/frame/source revision, then inspect the stage that rejected it. Apply the shared exact frontier before the input cap; do not fix unrelated clutter by raising every budget. Ready, genuinely incomplete coverage and ambiguous topology are different outcomes. Use a disposable fixture for cold-cache or resize experiments and preserve the authored placement.
- Contact cells can contain very close shared-edge samples. Ear occupancy uses signed geometric sides, not epsilon-expanded triangles that can block every valid ear. Preserve boundary identities/segments, positive area and atomic failure; a repeated ID introduced by welding is a topology defect, not permission to discard a positive-area cell or remove arbitrary vertices. Develop exact triangle coordinates with source-edge dot/cross products; subtracting nearly equal squared lengths can erase a real altitude. Reproduce both the failing cell and complete decal coverage, including each face of a wrapped corner, before claiming a contact fix.
- Keep cell existence, working triangulation and float publication separate. A surviving cell may need a strictly positive tip triangle below epsilon squared to retain shared endpoints. Cache winding uses half-ULP UV/source-barycentric intervals and affine parity; a float triangle collapsing to zero is not proof of corruption. Preserve metric/stretch plausibility and the malformed-fold/erased-UV tests, plus real saved-workflow cache round trips. Finite-cut and endpoint spatial indexes must match scalar predicates and ordering; do not obtain speed by removing contacts or reducing authored refinement. Endpoint proximity alone does not prove a new boundary sample: reject candidates exactly coincident with an existing corner in the source face's dominant orientation coordinates before insertion. Tiny normal-only jitter must not create duplicate projected vertices; retain even nextafter-distinct in-plane samples.
- Positive embedded area does not guarantee a usable length-only intrinsic metric: rounded lengths can erase a thin triangle's altitude. A uniform mollification can instead introduce large artificial cone curvature into a flat refined face. Use stable embedded areas/atan2 angles/face vectors and bounded MMP source windows. Carry each window's physical source-face heading with its scalar OLD/NEW ownership, freezing the first segment at a boundary or saddle pseudo-source; greedy traceback can cycle on genuine thin contact cells. Retain radius, interval/work/unfolding-depth limits, cancellation and finite checks. Source geometry, normals, barycentrics and the existing stretch/fold/overlap/connectivity checks remain authoritative. Refined paint boundaries retain sparse original-edge ancestry for working contact topology, while output and distortion checks use the actual fine triangles; remap that ancestry through any exact mixed-face proxy. Localize finite contact cuts with endpoint splits before their longitudinal cut. Preserve real contacts and ambiguity barriers. Do not substitute larger positional welding, discarded thin cells, planar projection or relaxed validation. Retain the curved needle, skinny-source metric and pointwise saved arch/painted-room/source-order/cache regressions.
- For paint UI delays, inspect transaction ownership at release and before menus/panels draw. After release, pending readback, surface preparation or optional material optimization must not retain editor commands; hover preparation never owns them. Preserve immediate release, one undoable stroke, exact cancellation and last-good presentation; do not remove unrelated transaction guards to hide a stuck paint owner.
- A GPU allocation failure after another preview or viewport closes may be lifetime corruption rather than genuine capacity exhaustion. Verify partial construction, invalid-handle destruction and a new owner's target growth. The canonical `forward/DecalGpu` wrapper uses `destroy_handle` so argument-dependent lookup cannot select `bgfx::destroy` ahead of a checked local template. Preserve the corresponding teardown/regrowth regression; increasing bgfx capacities is not a lifetime fix.
